CleanCalendar

A fast, clean desktop calendar for Windows, macOS and Linux, with two-way iCloud and Google Calendar sync.

What it does

  • Day, week, month and year views in the style of Apple Calendar.
  • Create, edit, move and delete events by clicking and dragging.
  • Keep separate calendars on your computer, and optionally connect iCloud, any other CalDAV server, or your Google account.
  • Works offline: changes are kept and sent when you're back online, and conflicting edits from other devices are merged instead of lost.

Why it asks for access to your Google Calendar

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you choose to connect, CleanCalendar asks for permission to see, edit, create and delete your Google calendars and events (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar). It uses that permission only to show your calendars and events in the app and to send the changes you make in the app back to Google.

Your calendar data goes directly between your computer and Google. CleanCalendar has no servers, does not collect analytics, and never shares your data with anyone. See the privacy policy for details.

Get it

CleanCalendar is open source. The code, downloads and build instructions are on GitHub.
