CleanCalendar

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Summary

CleanCalendar is a desktop app. Everything it stores stays on your computer. It has no servers, no user accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising. If you connect a Google, iCloud or other CalDAV account, your calendar data travels only between your computer and that service.

Information the app stores on your computer

  • The calendars and events you create or sync, and your display settings (such as calendar colors).
  • If you connect Google: a sign-in token issued by Google, which lets the app access your calendars without asking you to sign in again, and your Google account's email address, shown in the app's sidebar.
  • If you connect iCloud or another CalDAV server: the server address, your user name (for iCloud, your Apple ID), and the password you entered (for iCloud, an app-specific password).

This information is saved in files in your user profile's application-data and cache folders. Sign-in tokens and passwords are kept in your operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or the Secret Service on Linux) when one is available. None of it is sent anywhere else. Uninstalling the app and deleting those folders removes it.

Google user data

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you connect one, CleanCalendar requests the Google Calendar permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar and uses it only to:

  • read your calendar list and events so they can be shown in the app, and
  • create, update and delete events when you make those changes in the app.

CleanCalendar does not use your Google data for any other purpose. It does not transfer it to any third party, use it for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads your data. Calendar data received from Google is stored only on your computer, as described above.

CleanCalendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Location suggestions

Suggestions for an event's location are searched on your computer only; what you type is never sent anywhere. They come from location data for the countries you choose under Settings → Locations, which the app downloads from CleanCalendar's releases on GitHub. GitHub sees that download (which country, and your IP address) as it does any download, but not what you search for. The location data is OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors.

The map pin next to a location opens it in Google Maps in your browser, which sends that location to Google. This only happens when you click it.

iCloud and other CalDAV servers

Connecting an iCloud or other CalDAV account is optional. If you connect one, CleanCalendar signs in to that server with the user name and password you entered, and uses that access only to read your calendars and events and to create, update and delete events when you make those changes in the app. Your data and password are not sent to anyone else.

Removing access

You can disconnect any account at any time with Disconnect in the app's Accounts window, which deletes its token or password and its synced data from your computer. You can also revoke the app's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections, and revoke an iCloud app-specific password at account.apple.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
