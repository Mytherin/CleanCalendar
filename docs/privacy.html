CleanCalendar

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Summary

CleanCalendar is a desktop app. Everything it stores stays on your computer. It has no servers, no user accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising. If you connect a Google account, your calendar data travels only between your computer and Google.

Information the app stores on your computer

  • The calendars and events you create or sync, and your display settings (such as calendar colors).
  • If you connect Google: a sign-in token issued by Google, which lets the app access your calendars without asking you to sign in again, and your Google account's email address, shown in the app's sidebar.

This information is saved in a file in your user profile's application-data folder. It is not sent anywhere else. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes it.

Google user data

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you connect one, CleanCalendar requests the Google Calendar permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar and uses it only to:

  • read your calendar list and events so they can be shown in the app, and
  • create, update and delete events when you make those changes in the app.

CleanCalendar does not use your Google data for any other purpose. It does not transfer it to any third party, use it for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads your data. Calendar data received from Google is stored only on your computer, as described above.

CleanCalendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google at any time with Disconnect in the app's Google dialog, which deletes the token and synced Google data from your computer. You can also revoke the app's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be raised on GitHub.
